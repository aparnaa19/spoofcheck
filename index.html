spoofcheck federal agencies (BOD 18-01)

Can this domain be spoofed?

Checks whether a domain's DNS stops other people from sending email that claims to come from it.

Try: cisa.gov · github.com · example.com

How this works

SPF lists the servers allowed to send mail for a domain. It's checked against the envelope sender, an address recipients never see, so on its own it doesn't stop a forged From line.

DKIM signs each message so receivers can tell it came from the domain unaltered.

DMARC ties the two to the visible From address and tells receivers what to do when neither lines up: nothing (p=none), send to spam (p=quarantine) or refuse (p=reject). That's why the verdict is based mostly on DMARC.

If a subdomain has no DMARC record of its own, the parent domain's record applies. This page guesses the parent with a short built-in list of suffixes rather than the full Public Suffix List.

Lookups go from your browser to Google Public DNS, with Cloudflare as a fallback. Nothing else is sent anywhere. Source.
